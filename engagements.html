Post-merger integration of a Tier-1 energy acquisition

Sole signatory on Board-level strategic deliverables across six parallel workstreams. Synergy commitments validated and committed pre-close were defended through gateway reviews to Day 365.

Situation. A Tier-1 acquisition with a complex regulated and unregulated portfolio required a Strategy Management Office capable of governing six parallel integration workstreams comprising consultants from two of the world's leading firms. The acquirer needed a single accountable operator who could enforce gateway discipline rather than rubber-stamp consultant output.

What the practice did. Stood up the SteerCo cadence, defined the workstream charters, ran "Gateway Reviews" that rejected sub-par operating-model assumptions, and served as the sole signatory on every strategic paper before Board submission. Designed and operated the Day 0 readiness framework and the synergy-tracking model that linked workstream commitments to general-ledger lines.

Outcome. Synergy commitments preserved through to delivery; Board confidence held through the integration window; operating model implemented without rework.

Enterprise AI & automation reset, pre-acquisition readiness

Enterprise-wide AI and automation reset across IT, Finance, HR and Operations; Board-level governance, data controls and value-tracking framework supportive of subsequent Tier-1 diligence.

Situation. The organisation was preparing for a strategic transaction and required AI and automation to be governed, measured and defensible to a buyer's diligence team — not just present.

What the practice did. Designed the Board-level AI governance, the data control framework and the value-tracking framework. Linked every initiative to a named P&L line. Built the executive cadence that allowed the CEO and Audit & Risk Committee to read the AI portfolio in under 20 minutes per quarter.

Outcome. AI estate became an asset to the diligence rather than a liability. Annualised EBITDA improvement attributable to the programme was material and defensible at general-ledger level.
