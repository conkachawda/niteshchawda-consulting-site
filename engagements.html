McKinsey & Company × Alinta Energy / Integration

Making integration accountable.

Programme leadership · Governance · Operating model

The situation

At Alinta Energy, the challenge was to bring a complex acquisition into one operating model while protecting the value expected from the transaction.

My contribution

As a McKinsey-contracted engagement lead, I directed six parallel workstreams involving 25+ consultants, established gateway reviews, challenged operating-model assumptions and translated delivery choices into board-level decisions.

What changed

My contribution included governance and board papers supporting $240M+ in EBITDA outcomes across the wider programme, with explicit ownership and a disciplined connection between delivery and the value case.

Alinta Energy / AI & operations

Turning automation into operating value.

Enablement · Workflow redesign · Benefits tracking

The situation

Alinta Energy needed to modernise fragmented B2B and customer workflows and make AI and automation accountable to business performance.

My contribution

I connected process redesign with automation and machine-learning workflows, data controls, executive capability and financial value tracking.

What changed

The work removed duplicate activity and delivered an estimated $18M annual EBITDA uplift through automation and redesigned workflows.

Foxtel / Customer experience

Redesigning the customer experience.

Customer strategy · Conversational AI · Self-service

The situation

Foxtel needed to improve the service experience while reducing avoidable effort and cost.

My contribution

I shaped the customer-experience strategy and co-designed conversational AI, notifications and self-service journeys as parts of the wider operating model.

What changed

The customer-experience strategy delivered $40M in savings, alongside the Amelia AI chatbot, push notifications and self-service journeys. This was a contribution within a broader delivery team.

Carlyle & KKR / Private-equity diligence

Testing the value-creation thesis.

Diligence · AI economics · Investment case
