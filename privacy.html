About this site

This is the personal career portfolio of Nitesh Chawda, operated through Nitesh Chawda Consulting Pty Ltd. It presents professional experience, education and personal writing.

Information you choose to send

The contact form collects your name, email address, message and consent. These details are used to respond to your enquiry. Do not include confidential, sensitive or third-party personal information in an initial message.

Services used by this site

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The contact form uses Formspree to process submissions. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, and those providers receive normal connection information such as your IP address and browser details when their services are used.

Scheduling and social links take you to external websites with their own privacy practices. The website does not add an analytics or advertising tracking script.

Retention and your choices

Correspondence is kept as needed to respond, maintain relevant records and meet applicable obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided, email contact@niteshchawda.consulting.
