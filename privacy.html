Privacy notice.

How Nitesh Chawda Consulting Pty Ltd collects, uses and protects personal information.

Effective: May 2026 · Version: 1.0

This notice explains how Nitesh Chawda Consulting Pty Ltd (ACN [pending], "NCC", "we", "our") handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Where we engage with individuals located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or other regions with comparable regimes, we take reasonable steps to align our processing with the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation.

1. What we collect

  • Identity and contact data — name, role, organisation, email, phone, country.
  • Engagement data — the content of briefs, NDAs, contracts and correspondence.
  • Technical data — IP, device type, referrer and analytics events on this website (in aggregate).
  • Subscription data — if you subscribe to insights, your email address and stated preferences.

2. How we collect it

Directly from you when you contact us, brief us, engage us, attend a meeting, or subscribe to insights. From third parties only with your knowledge (for example, a referrer who has introduced you).

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the services we agree with you
  • To meet our legal, professional and contractual obligations
  • To operate this website and improve its content
  • To send insights only where you have opted in

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information to train machine-learning models. We do not place personal information into prompts or external AI services without explicit, documented authorisation.

4. Disclosure

We disclose personal information only to: (a) service providers under contract that obliges them to APP-equivalent standards (e.g. email, file storage, accounting); (b) regulators or courts where required by law; (c) parties you have authorised in writing.

5. International transfers

Our service providers are located in Australia, the United States and the European Union. Where data is transferred internationally we rely on contractual safeguards equivalent to APP 8.

6. Storage and security

Information is stored on encrypted services with multi-factor authentication and least-privilege access. Engagement records are retained for the period required to perform our services and meet our legal obligations (typically seven years from completion of an engagement).

7. Your rights

  • Access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdrawal of consent (where consent is the basis of processing)
  • Complaint to us in the first instance, then to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner

8. Cookies

This site uses essential cookies for navigation and a privacy-respecting analytics cookie to count visits in aggregate. We do not use cross-site tracking, advertising cookies or third-party fingerprinting.

9. Contact

Privacy queries: contact@niteshchawda.consulting with subject line "Privacy".

This notice is provided as part of our public materials and is not legal advice to any reader. We update it from time to time and the effective date above will reflect the latest version.
