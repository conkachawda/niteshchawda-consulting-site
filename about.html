§ 02 Operating principles

Three principles, applied without exception.

01Decisions, not decks.

A strategy that does not survive its first contact with a Board, a regulator, or an executive committee was never a strategy. The practice's deliverables are the calls themselves — written down clearly enough that they can be defended without their author in the room.

02Optionality is undervalued; optimisation is overrated.

Most enterprises optimise their way into fragility. The practice prefers to design for genuine flexibility — operating models, capital structures, and technology choices that preserve the ability to change direction when the world does.

03Independence is the product.

The practice will not accept implementation revenue, vendor commissions, or technology partner fees on engagements where it provides advice. There is one commercial relationship per engagement, and it is with the client.
