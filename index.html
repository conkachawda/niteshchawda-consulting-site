§ 05 Field notes

Brief observations from current and recent work. Published when there's something worth saying, and not before.

May 2026
"AI portfolios" tend to confuse activity with progress. The first useful question is not what should we build next — it is which existing initiative would survive a Board paper, written honestly, today.
Apr 2026
The cost of an autonomous workflow is not the LLM bill. It is the governance overhead of a system that takes decisions a human used to. Most agentic projects underprice this by an order of magnitude.
Mar 2026
Quantum-inspired optimisation has a frontier where it beats classical solvers. That frontier is narrower than vendors claim and broader than CIOs assume. The discipline is knowing which of your problems is a real candidate.
Feb 2026
A process worth automating is one whose output is a record. If the output is a judgement, it doesn't automate — it augments. The two need different operating models, different controls, and different KPIs.
