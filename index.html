§ 04 Field notes from the practice

Brief observations from current and recent work — published when there is something worth saying, and not before.

May 2026
"AI portfolios" tend to confuse activity with progress. The first useful question is not what should we build next — it is which existing initiative would survive a Board paper, written honestly, today.
Apr 2026
Synergies are not a slide. They are a balance-sheet item with an owner, an accounting treatment, and a date. The integrations that fail are the ones where none of those three is named.
Mar 2026
The Australian energy transition's bottleneck is not capital — capital is largely identified. It is operating models: who runs the assets, with what data, under what governance.
Feb 2026
A diligence calibrated for a hold reads differently from one calibrated for an IC paper. The questions that matter — culture, depth of management, real cost of integration — are usually the ones not asked.
