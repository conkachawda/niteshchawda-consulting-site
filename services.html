01 · AI & Automation Strategy

From AI as theatre to AI as operating model.

Most enterprises now have a portfolio of AI experiments and not much to show for them. The work that matters is the unglamorous part: the operating model that lets a CFO trust an agentic workflow with a P&L line, the governance that satisfies an Audit & Risk Committee, and the value-tracking discipline that makes the spend defensible to a Board.

Typical engagements

  • AI maturity diagnostic and target operating model (2–6 weeks)
  • Agentic workflow design and value-case (4–8 weeks)
  • AI governance and Board reporting framework (4 weeks)
  • Vendor and platform selection (independent of any vendor relationship)
  • Embedded "Head of AI" support during stand-up phase (3–6 months)

What good looks like

  • Every AI initiative tied to a named P&L line
  • A 90-day cadence the Board can read in under 20 minutes
  • A clear hand-off between strategy, build, and operate
02 · Post-Merger Integration

Day 0 is the easy day.

The integrations that fail are the ones that confuse activity with progress. The practice has completed McKinsey & Company's post-merger integration work stream as a contracted Engagement Manager — six parallel workstreams, twenty-five-plus consultants, sole signatory authority on Board-level deliverables — and brings that operating standard to mid-market and ASX-listed deals.

Typical engagements

  • Pre-close integration design (synergy thesis, Day 0 readiness, IMO standup)
  • 100-day plan with workstream charters and gateway reviews
  • SteerCo direction and challenge through Day 365
  • Carve-out support, including TSA design

What good looks like

  • Synergies tracked to general-ledger lines, not slide deck assertions
  • A single Integration Management Office with real authority
  • Gateway reviews that reject sub-par operating-model assumptions
03 · Private Equity Value Creation

Diligence is the cheapest part of the deal.

The practice supports sponsors and their portfolio companies on the decisions that drive multiple expansion: AI-enabled cost-out, technology-led revenue growth, and operating-model standardisation across the portfolio.

Typical engagements

  • Commercial and technology due diligence (4–6 weeks)
  • 100-day value-creation plan
  • Portfolio AI maturity assessment and prioritisation
  • Bolt-on integration playbook
  • Exit readiness and equity story refinement

What good looks like

  • A diligence calibrated for a hold, not an investment thesis
  • A 100-day plan written to be executed, not presented
  • Portfolio-wide insights that compound across companies
04 · Energy Transition & Future Grid

Capital decisions in a system that has not yet decided what it is.

The Australian and UK energy systems are pricing CAPEX, abatement and reliability against assumptions that are themselves moving. The practice supports incumbents and new entrants on Small Modular Reactor (SMR) economics, AI-enabled grid optimisation, regulated-asset strategy, and the operating-model implications of the transition.

Typical engagements

  • SMR / nuclear feasibility, including Monte Carlo CAPEX simulation
  • AI-enabled grid optimisation roadmap
  • Corporate & Institutional segment strategy
  • Energy operating-model design for the transition decade
  • Regulatory engagement support
05 · Board Advisory & NED

The seat between management and the shareholders.

The practice provides independent counsel to Boards and Audit & Risk Committees on strategy, technology adoption and capital allocation — and takes a small number of formal Non-Executive Director appointments per year.

Typical engagements

  • Independent strategic counsel (retainer)
  • Pre-Board paper challenge and quality review
  • Technology and AI governance for the Board
  • NED roles where the practice can add specific value (current: Simmonds Lumber, Strategy & Innovation)
  • Audit & Risk Committee briefings on emerging technology
06 · Quantitative & Quantum

Where the maths still earns its keep.

The practice maintains a working capability in quantitative methods that complement strategic work: macro-factor modelling, Monte Carlo simulation for CAPEX and policy decisions, and selected applications of quantum-inspired optimisation.

Typical engagements

  • Investment committee modelling support (Monte Carlo, scenario, sensitivity)
  • Macro factor and systematic strategy back-testing
  • Quantum-inspired optimisation pilots (logistics, energy dispatch, portfolio)
  • Quantitative challenge on existing client models
