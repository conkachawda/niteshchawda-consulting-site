02 / My approach

A simple path.
From learning to lasting value.

I’ve made my approach repeatable: build understanding, enable people, tackle the big rocks and prove the economics. Then build only what the evidence supports. Hover, tap or use the arrow keys to follow the roadmap.

01

Start with shared understanding.

Decision to progress

01 / Educate

Start with shared understanding.

Give leaders and teams a practical understanding of the technology, its possibilities and its limits.

02 / Enable

Turn learning into everyday capability.

Connect the learning to real roles, tools, workflows and safe-use guidance.

03 / Big rocks

Find the big rocks. Break them down.

Identify the constraints that matter, then turn each into smaller, testable opportunities.

04 / Business case

Make every idea earn its investment.

Test full costs, adoption, risk, time to value and sustained returns before committing.

05 / Scope & prove

Choose a boundary. Prove the value.

Select one area and test feasibility, adoption and economics against a clear baseline.

06 / Build

Build something people can depend on.

Combine the working solution with its controls, support and operating model.

07 / Expand

Extend what the evidence supports.

Take proven components into adjacent teams, checking the value in each new context.

08 / Scale

Make the value last.

Embed ownership, governance and benefits tracking so the returns endure.

The financial case runs through every stage. Proving value is a decision to continue, change direction or stop.

The financial discipline

One business case.
Revisited at every gate.

A promising demonstration is the start of a decision. The case becomes stronger as the assumptions meet reality.

Value

What changes?

Connect the idea to revenue, cost, capacity, service quality or risk. Establish the baseline and the person accountable for the benefit.

Investment

What does it take?

Include build and operating costs, change and training, integration, security and ongoing support. Test time to value and downside assumptions.

Evidence

What have we proved?

Compare actual adoption and outcomes with the original case. Refine, expand or stop based on the evidence, then keep measuring after launch.
