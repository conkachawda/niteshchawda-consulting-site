03
§ 03 / Emerging tech AI & Agentic Operating Models

From AI as theatre to AI as operating model.

Most enterprises now have a portfolio of AI experiments and not much to show for them. The work that matters is the unglamorous part: the operating model that lets a CFO trust an agentic workflow with a P&L line, the governance that satisfies an Audit & Risk Committee, and the value-tracking discipline that makes the spend defensible to a Board.

Typical engagements

  • AI maturity diagnostic and target operating model (2–6 weeks)
  • Agentic and multi-agent workflow design — including human-in-the-loop, escalation, and audit (4–8 weeks)
  • LLM and platform selection (independent of any vendor relationship)
  • AI governance and Board reporting framework (4 weeks)
  • Embedded "Head of AI" support during stand-up phase (3–6 months)

What good looks like

  • Every AI initiative tied to a named P&L line
  • A 90-day cadence the Board can read in under 20 minutes
  • A clear hand-off between strategy, build, and operate
04
§ 04 / Emerging tech Automation & Workflow Engineering

A process worth automating is one whose output is a record.

If the output is a judgement, it doesn't automate — it augments. The two need different operating models, different controls, and different KPIs. The practice helps clients tell them apart, build the ones that automate cleanly, and design the human-in-the-loop where automation alone is not enough.

Typical engagements

  • Process diagnostic — taxonomy of "automate / augment / leave alone"
  • RPA and LLM-orchestrated workflow design (full lifecycle)
  • Agentic stack architecture — where agents make calls a human used to
  • Operate-and-improve cadence after deployment (90-day, 180-day)
  • ROI defence — building the value-tracking the CFO will accept

§ 05 / Emerging tech Quantum & Optimisation Sciences

Where the maths still earns its keep.

Quantum-inspired optimisation has a frontier where it beats classical solvers. That frontier is narrower than vendors claim and broader than CIOs assume. The practice maintains a working capability in quantitative methods that complement the strategic work — and knows the difference between a problem that wants a solver and one that wants a heuristic.

Typical engagements

  • Investment Committee modelling support — Monte Carlo, scenario, sensitivity
  • Macro factor and systematic strategy back-testing in Python
  • Quantum-inspired optimisation pilots — energy dispatch, logistics, portfolio
  • Quantitative challenge on existing client models — pre-Board, pre-IC
  • Capability uplift for in-house quant and data-science teams

Nitesh is a Certified Quantum Specialist with the Australian Computer Society and serves as a subject matter expert for the Australian Quantum Alliance (Tech Council of Australia) and IEEE.
