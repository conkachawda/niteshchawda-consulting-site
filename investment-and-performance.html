When more investment
is not enough.

Liverpool Football Club: a study of strategy, people, investment and sustained performance.

All education & ideas
Setting
Harvard University
Module
MGMT 5515 · The Business of Football
Work type
Academic analysis · recommendations

I used Liverpool Football Club to explore a wider business question: how do strategy, investment decisions and people combine to create sustained performance?

The problem

Higher spending and rising enterprise value do not automatically translate into stronger operational performance. The organisation needed to be understood as both a business and a portfolio of complementary capabilities.

Three lenses on performance

Strategy and decision rights

I examined the relationship between corporate ambition, the operating model and accountability. A strategy needs clear authority for investment decisions and a common understanding of how performance will be judged.

Investment and complementary capabilities

Talent spending is a portfolio decision. Individual assets must work together, and the marginal value of further investment depends on the organisation’s existing strengths and gaps.

Analytics, judgement and culture

Analytical discipline can improve decisions, but it works alongside managerial judgement. Morale, trust and a shared sense of direction affect how those decisions take effect.

The central finding

Better outcomes require alignment between investment, accountability and the people doing the work. Culture and execution determine whether a strategy becomes performance.

How I developed the analysis

I connected corporate strategy, governance, workforce strategy and investment thinking using literature and public secondary material. The recommendations were academic analysis, not advice commissioned or implemented by the organisation studied.

Why it matters to my approach

Find the performance barrier, clarify the decisions, test the investment and enable the people. The question is how the parts work together to produce an outcome that lasts.
