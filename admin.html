Named, vetted assistants who learn your business and your preferences — not an anonymous ticket queue, and not an AI auto-reply pretending to be a person.

Real people in-market across Australia, the UK and the US. Work moves during your business day — promptly on an urgent email or a calendar change.

Confidentiality and careful handling of sensitive information, the way a seasoned executive assistant operates — calm, considered, never careless.

§ 02 How it works

Senior-set standards, delivered by people who care about the detail.

One accountable point of contact scopes the work, sets the standard, and stays responsible for the outcome. The work itself is delivered by vetted executive-support professionals across Australia, the UK and the US, matched to you and the way you like to work.

It means you get the discipline of a senior operator setting up the engagement — clear scope, defined standards, a single line of accountability — with the warmth and continuity of a real person who learns your rhythm. No long-term headcount, no recruitment overhead, no fixed seat to keep full.

§ 04 Why the model works

We don't just place an assistant. We stay accountable for how they perform.

Most agencies introduce a candidate and disappear. Every assistant we place is vetted, trained to our standard, and actively managed — personally.

Talk to us
  • 01
    Rigorously vetted
    Multi-stage selection — screening, reference and background checks, and a practical skills assessment. Only a small share make it through.
  • 02
    Trained to our standard
    Executive communication, calendar and inbox mastery, confidentiality and data handling, and the core tool stack — Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace — before they ever touch your work.
  • 03
    Actively managed, not just placed
    I hold weekly stand-ups with every assistant, monitor performance, and coach them. Any issue comes straight to me — no account-manager layer, no finger-pointing.
  • 04
    Continuity, covered
    If your assistant is ever unavailable, we arrange cover. You're never left without support.
Our guarantee

If the fit isn't right, we replace your assistant free within the first 30 days. Not satisfied in the first two weeks? You don't pay.

We also deliberately recruit and develop exceptional people returning from career breaks, parental leave, and hardship — motivated, loyal, genuinely capable support for you, and real opportunity created. Good business and good values, in the same hire.

§ 05 Human + agentic

People carry the judgement. Agents carry the load.

The same support, twice the leverage. Every engagement pairs a vetted human assistant with the automations and AI agents we build around them — so the human hours go to judgement, relationships and discretion, and the repeatable work runs itself.

Talk to us
  • 01
    Human support
    A vetted, trained, managed assistant who learns your business — handling the calls, the calendar judgement, the sensitive correspondence and the relationships only a person can hold.
  • 02
    Agentic support — built for you
    We design and build the AI agents, bots and automations that sit around your assistant: inbox triage, scheduling, data entry, reporting, document drafting — the repeatable load, handled, so your human hours compound.
  • 03
    The result — scale without the headcount
    One assistant, amplified. You get the output of a small team from a single accountable point of contact, because the agents do the heavy lifting and the human does the thinking.

§ 08 Engagement models

Support that flexes with your life.

You don't hire a seat and hope to fill it. You tell us what you need, and we match you with the right support — scaling up or down as life and the business move.

01Fractional support

A set number of hours each week against a defined scope. Ideal for founders and executives who need consistent support without a full-time hire.

02Embedded assistant

A full-time-equivalent seat dedicated to you, working inside your systems and rhythm — without the overhead of recruiting and carrying the role.

03Project burst

A defined piece of work — a migration, a backlog, an event, a launch — scoped, resourced and delivered to a deadline.

04Surge & overflow

On-demand support when your own team is at the limit. A trusted bench you can call on for the busy weeks, then stand down.
